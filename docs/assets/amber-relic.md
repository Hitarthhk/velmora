# Amber relic emblem

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. No brand name, wordmark, letters or original identity assets are used.

## Final generation prompt

Create ONE extraordinary original emblem for a cinematic premium whisky website, no brand name, no letters, no typography, no wordmark. Not a generic minimalist mountain icon. Art direction: a surreal fractured amber relic, an impossible continuous sculptural ribbon of polished antique gold and translucent deep whisky amber twisting into a compact vertical diamond / teardrop silhouette; central negative space cuts a razor-sharp jagged Himalayan peak through the jewel; a thin broken orbital halo slices diagonally around the central sculpture with one small suspended amber satellite. Asymmetric but balanced, edgy luxury editorial identity, precise beveled metal facets, rich emerald-black recessed shadow, bright champagne edges and copper caustics. Strong memorable silhouette, sculptural and mysterious rather than decorative crest, no animals, no leaves, no bottles, no shields, no generic monogram. Center the complete standalone emblem with generous empty margin all around, emblem occupies 80% of square composition, transparent background, no cast shadow outside the object, no backdrop, no mockup. This is the final production logo image for a dark forest-green website; distinct large shapes should remain clear at 64 pixels. Finish like a beautifully art-directed photorealistic 3D jewellery object, crisp boundaries, premium metal material, restrained micro-detail. Transparent cutout.

## Assets
