## Final generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: cinematic website environment plate, landscape 16:9, high resolution. Create a distinct intimate Himalayan valley at early golden sunrise, viewed from inside the valley rather than a wide summit panorama. Layered dark blue-grey mountain ridges frame both sides, distant snow peaks sit high in the upper third, low thin clouds and mist recede into the valley. Gentle natural amber sunlight from the upper left grazes ridge edges. The center and lower-middle area must be calm, dark, low-detail atmospheric negative space for a separately composited premium whisky bottle and readable product copy, with believable photographic shadows and restrained warmth. The valley should feel deep, luxurious, and realistic, like a cinematic mountain documentary still. Fine rock and conifer textures mostly near the outer edges; keep the central label area clean. No product, no bottle, no glassware, no logo, no text, no people, no buildings, no particles, no artificial beams, no lens flare, no white flash, no illustration, no flat green backdrop. Maintain realistic mountain scale and natural light; avoid an overbright sun disk. This is an environment only; original brand assets will be composited in code.
